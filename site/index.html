Patrick Isopp

Founder · Actor · Filmmaker

Patrick Isopp is an Austrian actor, writer and filmmaker. He graduated from the Film Academy Vienna and completed his MA in Acting (Classical) at the Royal Central School of Speech and Drama in London in 2026, supported by the Embassy Theatre Scholarship.

Since graduating in Vienna he has worked across Austria and Germany on screen and stage. His credits include the Netflix series Totenfrau, SOKO Donau and the ORF TV film Bis in die Seele ist mir kalt, as well as productions at Theater INC. Darmstadt, Junge Luisenburg and klagenfurter ensemble. In London he played Sir Toby Belch in Twelfth Night. Behind the camera, he works as a videographer, editor and writer. He has published short stories and a book (The Room / Das Zimmer, 2024), produced the short films No Heroes and Tunnel of Horror (2022) and directed the short play Confabulations (2026).

Away from set, he is passionate about movement and sport — from judo and BJJ to long-distance running and cycling — always with a new challenge on the horizon.
